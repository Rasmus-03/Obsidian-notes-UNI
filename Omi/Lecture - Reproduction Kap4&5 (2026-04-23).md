## 🔑 Key Topics

### Hvorfor sex? (Why sex?)
- Sexual reproduction has significant **costs** — especially to males
- The **twofold cost of sex**: females investing in males who don't directly produce offspring is evolutionarily expensive
- Sexual reproduction persists despite costs → must have evolutionary benefits

### Costs of Sexual Reproduction
- High energetic and resource costs allocated to males
- **Asymmetric investment** between sexes (males vs. females)
- Costs include time, energy, and risk (e.g. finding a mate, mate competition)

### Parent–Offspring Relationship
- The **genetic relationship between parents and offspring** plays a role in reproductive strategies
- Trade-offs between number of offspring and investment per offspring

### Key Concepts (from slide OCR)
- **Ukendt** (unknown) selective pressures driving sex
- Only a fraction of organisms are "deep" reproducers
- Evolutionary costs for males are central to understanding sexual selection
