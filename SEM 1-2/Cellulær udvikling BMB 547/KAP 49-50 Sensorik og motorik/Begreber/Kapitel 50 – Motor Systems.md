# Kapitel 50 – Motor Systems

> [!tip] **For en 5-årig:**  
> See "Kapitel 50 – Muskler og bevægelse.md" for full note in Danish. Motor systems are how the brain controls movement – from thinking "move!" to muscles actually contracting.

## Key Points
- **3 muscle types**: skeletal (voluntary), cardiac (involuntary), smooth (involuntary)
- **Sarcomere**: basic contractile unit between Z-discs
- **Sliding filament theory**: myosin pulls actin → sarcomere shortens
- **Cross-bridge cycle**: myosin binds actin → power stroke → ATP releases
- **EC coupling**: action potential → Ca²⁺ release → contraction
- **Motor unit**: one motor neuron + all fibers it innervates
