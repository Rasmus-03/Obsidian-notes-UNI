# Det autonome nervesystem\

> [!INFO] **Definition**
> CHAPTER 55  Energy Flow and Chemical Cycling in Ecosystems 1297
Conservation of Energy
Cells transform energy and matter, subject to the laws of 
thermodynamics (see Concept 6.1). Cell biologists study 
these transformations within organelles and cells, and mea-
sure the amounts of energy and chemical compounds that 
cross the cells’ boundaries. Ecosystem ecologists do the same 
thing, except in their case the “cell” is an entire ecosystem.

> **Kilde:** Campbell Biology, 12th edition

Det autonome nervesystem (ANS): Den del af det perifere nervesystem, der regulerer ubevidste funktioner som hjerterytme, fordøjelse, respiration. Består af sympatikus, parasympatikus og enterisk nervesystem.
