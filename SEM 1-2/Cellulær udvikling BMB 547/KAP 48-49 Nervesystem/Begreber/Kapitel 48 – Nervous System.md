# Kapitel 48 – Nervous System

> [!tip] **For en 5-årig:**  
> The nervous system is the body's super-fast communication network. The brain is headquarters, the spinal cord is the main cable, and nerves are wires carrying electrical messages everywhere.

## Key Points
- **CNS**: brain (cerebrum, cerebellum, brainstem) + spinal cord
- **PNS**: sensory (body → CNS) + motor (CNS → body)
- **Autonomic**: sympathetic (fight/flight) + parasympathetic (rest/digest)
- **Resting membrane potential**: -70 mV (K⁺ inside, Na⁺ outside, Na⁺/K⁺ pump)
- **Action potential**: depolarization (Na⁺ in) → repolarization (K⁺ out) → hyperpolarization
- **Synapse**: chemical transmission via neurotransmitters
