# Immunohistokemi

> [!INFO] **Definition**
> Immunohistochemistry (IHC) is a technique that uses antibodies to detect specific antigens in tissue sections, enabling visualization of protein expression and localization in cells.

> **Kilde:** Campbell Biology, 12th edition

Immunohistokemi (IHC): En teknik, der bruger antistoffer til at detektere specifikke proteiner i vævssnit. Antistoffer er mærket med enzymer eller fluoroforer → farvereaktion eller fluorescens. Anvendes i diagnostik (fx cancer-klassificering, nyrebiopsi), og forskning.
