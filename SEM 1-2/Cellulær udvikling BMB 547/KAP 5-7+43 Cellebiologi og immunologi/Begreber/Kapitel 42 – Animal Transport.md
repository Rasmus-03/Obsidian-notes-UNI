# Kapitel 42 – Animal Transport

> [!tip] **For en 5-årig:**  
> Animals need a transport system to carry oxygen, food and waste around. It's like a city's underground metro – the heart is the central station, blood vessels are the tunnels, and blood cells are the passengers.

## Key Points
- **Open vs closed circulatory systems**: insects have open; vertebrates have closed
- **Heart**: 4-chambered (2 atria, 2 ventricles) → double circulation
- **Systemic circulation**: oxygenated blood → body → deoxygenated blood back
- **Pulmonary circulation**: deoxygenated blood → lungs → oxygenated blood back
- **Blood components**: plasma (55%), RBCs (O₂ transport), WBCs (immunity), platelets (clotting)
- **Cardiac cycle**: systole (contraction) → diastole (relaxation)
