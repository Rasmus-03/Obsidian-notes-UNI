# Cytochrom P450

> [!INFO] **Definition**
> Cytochrome P450 enzymes are a family of heme-containing monooxygenases that catalyze the oxidation of organic substances, playing a key role in drug metabolism and steroid synthesis.

> **Kilde:** Campbell Biology, 12th edition

Cytochrom P450 (CYP): En stor familie af enzymer (monooxygenaser), primært i leveren, der er involveret i metabolisme af lægemidler, toksiner og endogene stoffer (steroidhormoner, fedtsyrer). Kaldes P450 pga. maksimal absorbans ved 450 nm. CYP-aktiviteten varierer genetisk, hvilket påvirker lægemiddelmetabolisme.
