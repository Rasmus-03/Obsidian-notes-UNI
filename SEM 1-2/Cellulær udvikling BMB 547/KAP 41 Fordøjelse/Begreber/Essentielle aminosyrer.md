Essential Amino Acids
All organisms require a standard set of 20 amino acids to make 
a complete set of proteins (see Figure 5.14). Plants and micro-
organisms normally can produce all 20. Most animals have 
the enzymes to synthesize about half of these amino acids, as 
long as their diet includes sulfur and organic nitrogen.
