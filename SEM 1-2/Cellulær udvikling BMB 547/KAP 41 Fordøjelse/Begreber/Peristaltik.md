Other Types of Muscle
Although all muscles share the same fundamental mecha-
nism of contraction—actin and myosin filaments sliding 
past each other—there are many different types of muscle. Vertebrates, for example, have cardiac muscle and smooth 
muscle in addition to skeletal muscle (see Figure 40.5). Vertebrate cardiac muscle is found only in the heart 
and, like skeletal muscle, is striated.
