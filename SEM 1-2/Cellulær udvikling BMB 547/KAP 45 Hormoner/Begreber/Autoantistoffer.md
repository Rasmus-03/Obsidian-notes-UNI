# Autoantistoffer

> [!INFO] **Definition**
> CHAPTER 47  Animal Defenses Against Infection 1107
B cell antigen receptors and antibodies bind to intact anti-
gens in the blood and lymph. As illustrated in Figure 47.10b 
for antibodies, they can bind to antigens on the surface of 
pathogens or free in body fluids. Antigen Recognition by T Cells
For a T cell, the antigen receptor consists of two different 
polypeptide chains, an a chain and a b chain, linked by a 
disulfide bridge (Figure 47.11).

> **Kilde:** Campbell Biology, 12th edition

Autoantistoffer: Antistoffer produceret af immunsystemet, der fejlagtigt angriber kroppens egne væv. Involveret i autoimmune sygdomme som Graves' sygdom (stimulerer TSH-receptor), type 1-diabetes (ødelægger β-celler) og leddegigt.
