# Kapitel 41 – Animal Hormones

> [!tip] **For en 5-årig:**  
> Hormones are the body's messengers – travelling in blood to tell cells what to do. Only cells with the right receptor (mailbox) can read the message.

## Key Points
- **3 hormone types**: peptide (hydrophilic), steroid (lipophilic), amine (varies)
- **3 major axes**: HPA (stress), HPT (metabolism), HPG (reproduction)
- **Feedback loops**: negative (most hormones), positive (oxytocin in birth)
- **Endocrine glands**: hypothalamus, pituitary, thyroid, adrenal, pancreas, gonads
