# Kapitel 46 – Animal Reproduction

> [!tip] **For en 5-årig:**  
> Reproduction is how animals make more of themselves. Some just split in two (asexual), while others need a male and a female (sexual). The advantage of sex? Mixing genes creates variation – like shuffling a deck of cards.

## Key Points
- **Asexual reproduction**: budding, fragmentation, parthenogenesis – clones
- **Sexual reproduction**: gametes (sperm + egg) → zygote → genetic variation
- **Male system**: testes (sperm), epididymis (storage), penis (delivery)
- **Female system**: ovaries (eggs), oviduct (fertilization), uterus (gestation), vagina
- **Hormonal control**: HPG axis (GnRH → FSH/LH → sex hormones)
- **Menstrual cycle**: follicular phase (estrogen dominates) → ovulation → luteal phase (progesterone dominates)
