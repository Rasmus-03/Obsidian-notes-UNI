# Kapitel 44 – Osmoregulation

> [!tip] **For en 5-årig:**  
> Osmoregulation is how the body keeps the right balance of water and salt. The kidneys are the main regulators – like a smart water filter that decides what to keep and what to flush.

## Key Points
- **Osmoregulation**: maintaining water and salt balance
- **Kidney structure**: cortex (outer), medulla (inner)
- **Nephron**: functional unit (~1 million/kidney)
- **3 processes**: filtration (glomerulus), reabsorption (tubules), secretion (tubules)
- **Countercurrent multiplier**: Henle's loop creates osmotic gradient
- **ADH**: inserts aquaporins → water reabsorption → concentrated urine
- **RAAS**: renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system → Na⁺ reabsorption → BP ↑

## Exam Training
- Q: "Explain the countercurrent multiplier" → descending: H₂O out; ascending: NaCl out → gradient
- Q: "How does ADH affect urine?" → more aquaporins → more concentrated urine
