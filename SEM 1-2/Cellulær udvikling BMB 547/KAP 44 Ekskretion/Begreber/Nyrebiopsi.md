In humans, the excretory system consists 
of kidneys, a pair of organs each about 
10 cm in length, as well as organs for trans-
porting and storing urine. Urine produced 
by each kidney exits through a duct called 
the ureter; the two ureters drain into a 
common sac called the urinary bladder. During urination, urine is expelled from the 
bladder through a tube called the urethra, 
which empties to the outside near the vagina 
in females and through the penis in males.
